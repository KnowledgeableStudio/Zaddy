The story behind the music.

My name is iZad Casanova, and I was born in New York City on November 11, 1989. I'm a first-generation American, still living in the Bronx, and still chasing a dream. Life hasn't made things easy — but I've kept pushing forward.

Music is my therapy, and my mission is bigger than me. What follows is the real story — struggle, faith, family, and the reason behind every verse.
